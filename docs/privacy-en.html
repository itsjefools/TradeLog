Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TradeLog ("Service") handles personal information of its users.

1. Information We Collect
(1) Account information: email address, username, display name, profile image, bio, nationality, trade style
(2) Trade information: trade data you enter (currency pair, P&L, memos, etc.)
(3) Posts, comments, likes, bookmarks, reposts, follows
(4) Device information: OS, device type, push notification token
(5) Subscription information: status only (payment info is managed by Apple/Google; we do not store it)
(6) Reports and block records

2. Purpose of Use
(1) Providing, operating, and improving the Service
(2) User support and inquiry response
(3) Sending push notifications
(4) Preventing abuse and investigating violations
(5) Creating anonymized statistics

3. Third-Party Sharing
We do not share collected information with third parties without User consent, except:
(1) When required by law
(2) When necessary to protect life, body, or property and consent is difficult to obtain
(3) When sharing with service vendors under confidentiality agreements

4. Service Vendors
The Service uses:
- Supabase: database, authentication, storage
- Expo / EAS: push notifications, build
- RevenueCat: subscription management
- Apple App Store / Google Play: app distribution and billing

5. Data Storage
Collected data is stored with appropriate security measures.

6. Your Rights
You have the right to:
(1) Request disclosure, correction, or deletion of your personal information
(2) Delete your account (available from in-app Settings)

Deleting your account permanently removes all associated posts, trade records, comments, etc.

7. Push Notifications
Push notification permission can be changed at any time in your device settings.

8. Cookies
The Service is a mobile app and does not use cookies. It uses local storage (device-side session etc.).

9. Minors
Users under 13 may not use the Service.

10. Policy Changes
This Policy may be updated as needed. Updated versions take effect upon posting within the Service.
